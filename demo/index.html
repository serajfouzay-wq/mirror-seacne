How it runs at your event

Guests scan a QR code

They type their name and take a selfie on their own phone, straight from your registration or RSVP page.

You approve photos

Each approved photo pops up on the big screen with the guest's name, then joins the wall.

One tap forms the picture

From the admin panel you choose a letter, a name, a number, a shape or your logo, and every photo flies into place.
